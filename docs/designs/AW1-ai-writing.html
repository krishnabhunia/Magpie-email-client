AI writing

Show "What the reply says"New
Magpie suggests Yes / No / … from the email; you can change it before the draft is written.

How it sounds

Defaults for every draft. You can change any of them for one draft from the AI panel.

Formality New
Like a chatLike a letter
On upgrade: "Formal" and "Informal" move out of Tone into the new Formality scale. A saved Tone of Formal becomes Tone: Professional + Formality: Formal; Informal becomes Tone: Friendly + Formality: Casual. Nothing else you saved changes.

Culture & etiquette New

How greetings, names and directness are handled. "Auto" follows what the other person wrote.

Remember my choices per contactOptional
When you change the style for someone (e.g. Very formal + Japanese etiquette for one client), Magpie uses it next time you write to that address. Kept on this PC.

Rules that stay the same

SrNo.RuleHow
1Sending is always yoursThe draft only goes into the editor when you click Use draft; nothing is sent by the AI.
2Recipients never come from the AIReply/Reply All recipients are calculated locally; Forward's To is whatever you type.
3Choices in the panel are for this draft only"Save as my defaults" is the only way they change Settings (staged until Apply, as today).
4Your typed text is safeIf you've typed, Use draft asks: Append · Replace · Keep editing. Signature and quoted/forwarded block are never touched.
5Privacy and consentBounded email text; attachment names only; per-feature consent; email text treated as untrusted data; no invented facts or promises beyond what you put in "What the reply says".
6Stale results droppedSwitching email, account or AI off cancels pending writes, as in 10.0.0.
7One request per clickStance chips rewrite at once; other changes collect and wait for Rewrite, so the provider isn't called on every click.

Questions before I build · answered: approved 30 Oct 2026 with the recommended answers

1 · Formal / Informal leaves the Tone list.Tone and Formality would otherwise contradict each other (Tone: Informal + Formality: Very formal). I'd migrate saved values as shown on screen 03.Recommended: move them into Formality.
2 · Forward when To is still empty.Write the note straight away with "Hi," and swap only the greeting when you add someone, or wait until a recipient is added.Recommended: write at once, update the greeting later.
3 · Culture lists.English style: Match sender · Indian · British · American · Global plain. Etiquette: Neutral · Indian · Japanese · German · Middle East · US. Add or remove any?Recommended: these lists; Custom instructions cover the rest.
4 · Remember per contact.Useful for clients who need a fixed style; adds a small table on this PC.Recommended: include it, Off by default.
5 · Mac.The prompt and settings live in shared code, so Mac gets them; the new panel UI is Windows-only in this PR (as with FR1).Recommended: Windows UI now, Mac panel as a later item.
6 · Do you have any lacunas, questions, blockers or doubts?If not, the recommended answers above apply and I'll open the draft PR.
