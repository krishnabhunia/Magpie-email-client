Preview ready. Labels start expanded.
01 / ONE CONTROL

Collapse just Labels.

Click anywhere on the Labels heading, including the arrow. Inbox, Sent, Drafts, All Mail, Spam and Trash remain visible inside the account.

  • Every custom label goes inside this section.
  • The small number is the number of labels, not a sum of unread emails.
02 / KEEP YOUR PLACE

Reading continues.

When the open label is hidden, a short “Viewing” line keeps its name visible. “Show labels” reveals the list without changing your message selection.

  • Clicking a label still opens it normally.
  • Individual nested-label behavior stays as it is.
03 / REMEMBER THE CHOICE

Each account remembers.

Store this section’s expanded state locally for each Gmail account. Keep it through refresh, sync and restart. Existing users begin with Labels expanded.

  • Enter or Space operates the heading.
  • A visible focus ring and announced expanded state support keyboard and screen-reader use.
Saved data & side effects. The app would save only a local expanded/collapsed preference per account. Gmail labels, their membership, counts, sync, standard-folder placement and the selected reading view stay intact. This HTML uses fictional messages and stores only its own demo expansion choices in this browser tab. No mail, sign-in, provider or network request is made by the prototype.
Alternative considered. Collapsing the existing account header also hides its standard folders. The recommended separate Labels heading keeps those folders available. Individual label-branch changes were explicitly excluded from the requested design.
