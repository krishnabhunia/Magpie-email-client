Gmail labels that fold

Krishna, 30 Oct 2026: "The labels in the Gmail are not collapsible in Magpie view." Pick one option (A, B or C). Nothing gets built until you approve. Click the ▾ arrows and the group headings in the mock-ups below; they open and close.

Why the labels don't fold today

Magpie can already fold a folder's subfolders. A parent gets an arrow only when it is a real folder. When a Gmail label is made inside another one (for example "Clients/Acme"), Gmail may report the parent "Clients" as a container only (no mail of its own; IMAP \Noselect). Magpie skips those containers, so "Acme" loses its parent. It then shows at the top level as a plain row, with no arrow.

What Gmail sends (IMAP LIST)

(\HasNoChildren)            "INBOX"
(\Noselect \HasChildren)    "[Gmail]"
(\HasNoChildren \Sent)      "[Gmail]/Sent Mail"
(\Noselect \HasChildren)    "Clients"        ← skipped
(\HasNoChildren)            "Clients/Acme"
(\HasNoChildren)            "Clients/Globex"
(\HasChildren)              "Travel"
(\HasNoChildren)            "Travel/2026"

What Magpie shows now

"Travel" folds because it is a real label. "Acme" and "Globex" lose their parent and sit in the top list as single words: you can't tell they belong together, and nothing folds them away. With 30–60 labels the account's list gets very long.

Not verified: I can't see your mailbox. If your labels look different from this, a screenshot of the sidebar would confirm the cause.
